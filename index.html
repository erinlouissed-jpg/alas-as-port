Simala

My Visit to Simala Church

We visited Simala Church last June 12, 2025. It was not my first time to visit, but I was still amazed by how it looked. This was my second time to see the inside, since in previous years it was still under renovation.

Seeing the messages, prayers, and hopes of the people felt truly uplifting and gave me a deep sense of peace. Everyone visiting Simala carries a spirit of hope and serenity, making the whole place even more inspiring.

Simala Church is not only majestic in appearance but also miraculous in spirit, a place where faith, hope, and answered prayers come together.
